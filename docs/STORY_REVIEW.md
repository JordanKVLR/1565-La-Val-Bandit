## Battle length

Ordinary battles now end only when every enemy is beaten (or a named leader falls). A round limit remains only where the story sets a deadline: The Admiral's Battery ("until the morning"), The Hospital Tents and Rearguard of the Sick ("until the army returns" / "until the sick reach the shore"). Beating every enemy still wins those too. b2 and a6 lost one enemy each so that every battle stays winnable without a timer.
